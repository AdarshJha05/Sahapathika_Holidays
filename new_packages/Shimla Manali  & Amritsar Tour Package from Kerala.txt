Introduction

Experience the best of the Himalayas and Punjab with this thoughtfully planned Himachal and Amritsar tour package from Kerala. 5. Day-wise Itinerary

Day 1: Delhi to Shimla – Journey into the Himalayas

Meals: DinnerOvernight: Shimla

Arrive at Delhi Airport or Railway Station, where you will meet the representative and begin your road journey towards Shimla.

The drive gradually leaves the plains behind as the landscape transforms into the pine- and forest-covered hills of Himachal Pradesh. On reaching Shimla, check in to your hotel and settle in.

The evening is free for relaxation or independent exploration depending on your arrival time. Later, enjoy dinner and spend the night in Shimla.

Day 2: Kufri & Shimla – Mountain Views and Colonial Charm

Meals: Breakfast & DinnerOvernight: Shimla

After breakfast, head towards Kufri, one of the popular mountain destinations around Shimla. Its elevated location and Himalayan surroundings make it a favourite for enjoying scenic views and seasonal outdoor activities.

Return towards Shimla and spend time exploring the famous Mall Road and The Ridge, the lively heart of the hill station. You can enjoy the atmosphere, browse local shops and take in views of the surrounding mountains.

The Christ Church and the historic library building around The Ridge add to Shimla's distinctive colonial character.

Return to the hotel for dinner and overnight stay.

Day 3: Shimla to Manali via Mandi & Kullu

Meals: Breakfast & DinnerOvernight: Manali

After an early breakfast, check out and begin the scenic road journey from Shimla to Manali.

The route takes you through the landscapes around Sundernagar, Mandi and Pandoh, with opportunities to see places such as Sundernagar Lake, Pandoh Dam and Hanogi Mata Temple along the way.

Continue towards the Kullu region, where you will also visit a Kullu Shawl Factory.

An optional river rafting experience on the Beas River at Kullu may be available, subject to weather conditions and payable directly by guests.

Continue to Manali and check in at your hotel. Dinner and overnight stay.

Day 4: Solang Valley – Himalayan Scenery & Adventure

Meals: Breakfast & DinnerOvernight: Manali

After breakfast, travel to Solang Valley, one of the most popular excursion destinations near Manali.

Surrounded by dramatic mountain scenery, Solang offers beautiful Himalayan views and is known for a range of seasonal adventure activities. Guests can enjoy the landscape and, where available, participate in activities at their own expense.

After exploring the valley, return to Manali for an evening at leisure.

Dinner and overnight stay at the hotel.

Important: Rohtang Pass sightseeing is not included in this package. Access and transportation are subject to applicable regulations, local taxi-union policies and prevailing charges. Guests wishing to visit Rohtang Pass can arrange the required local transportation directly at their own cost.

Day 5: Manali Local Sightseeing

Meals: Breakfast & DinnerOvernight: Manali

After breakfast, explore some of Manali's well-known local attractions.

The sightseeing includes Hadimba Temple, Manu Temple, Tibetan Monastery, Club House and Van Vihar. Together, these places offer a glimpse into Manali's spiritual traditions, local culture, natural surroundings and relaxed mountain lifestyle.

Later, enjoy free time to explore the local market or take a leisurely walk around Mall Road.

Return to the hotel for dinner and overnight stay.

Day 6: Manali to Amritsar – From the Mountains to Punjab

Meals: Breakfast & DinnerOvernight: Amritsar

After breakfast, check out and begin the long road journey from Manali towards Amritsar.

The approximately 390-kilometre journey takes you from the Himalayan region towards the plains of Punjab and can take around 9–10 hours depending on road and traffic conditions.

On arrival in Amritsar, check in to the hotel and relax after the journey.

Dinner and overnight stay at Amritsar.

Day 7: Golden Temple & Attari-Wagah Border

Meals: Breakfast & DinnerOvernight: Amritsar

Begin the day with a visit to the magnificent Golden Temple, one of India's most revered Sikh pilgrimage destinations and the spiritual centre of Amritsar.

Continue to Jallianwala Bagh, an important historical site, followed by time for local shopping.

Later in the afternoon, proceed towards the Attari-Wagah Border to witness the famous lowering-of-the-flags ceremony. The highly coordinated ceremony performed by the security forces of India and Pakistan has become one of the region's best-known visitor experiences.

Return to Amritsar after the ceremony.

Dinner and overnight stay at the hotel.

Day 8: Amritsar to Delhi – Departure

Meals: Breakfast

After breakfast, check out from the hotel and proceed towards Delhi.

The road journey is approximately 450 kilometres and may take around 8–9 hours depending on traffic and road conditions.

As per the travel requirement, the journey can conclude with a drop at Delhi or at Amritsar Airport / Railway Station.

Your Himachal–Amritsar holiday comes to an end with memories of Himalayan landscapes, mountain towns, spiritual landmarks and Punjab's vibrant culture.

9. Best Time to Visit

The ideal travel period depends on what you want to experience.

Spring & Summer: Pleasant conditions make sightseeing in Shimla, Manali and surrounding areas attractive.

Monsoon: Mountain roads can be affected by rain, so travellers should allow flexibility for road conditions and journey times.

Winter: Shimla and Manali can offer a distinctly cold Himalayan experience, with the possibility of snow in suitable areas. Weather can also influence road travel and access to higher-altitude locations.

For the most comfortable experience, choose dates after considering the prevailing mountain weather and your preferred activities.
